Context

Throughput uses cores × clock × instructions per cycle. That model describes scalar CPUs well and GPUs badly: for machines whose core count runs into the millions, the figure counts GPU lanes against a CPU clock and should be read as an index, not a measurement.

Costs are the price of the machine as configured, in the dollars of its launch year. They are not inflation-adjusted, so a 1977 price and a 2026 price are not directly comparable.
